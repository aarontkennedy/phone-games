ENABLE word list (the word games' list of words, src/core/words/enable.txt)
Enhanced North American Benchmark LExicon, by Alan Beale and M. Cooper
License: Public domain

From its readme: "The ENABLE master word list, WORD.LST, is herewith formally
released into the Public Domain. Anyone is free to use it or distribute it in
any manner they see fit. [...] Game designers may feel free to incorporate the
WORD.LST into their games. Please mention the source and credit us as
originators of the list."

This app keeps the list's words of 3 to 8 letters, minus slurs and vulgar
words (scripts/make-words.mjs). The word list stays in the public domain: this
app's license doesn't restrict copying or sharing it.
